Research agenda

My research agenda focuses on electoral politics, political communication, political institutions, and political behavior, with an emphasis on communication strategies from political elites and how voters understand and process these communications.

I assemble large, novel datasets and use machine learning and natural language processing for text classification and quantitative text analysis. My recent work also examines large language models for text generation, annotation, and classification tasks in political science.
