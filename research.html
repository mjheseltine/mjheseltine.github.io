Research agenda

My research agenda focuses on electoral politics, broadly defined, with an emphasis on communication strategies from political elites and how voters understand and process these communications.

Methodologically, I assemble large, novel datasets and use machine learning and natural language processing for text classification. My current post-doctoral research focuses on the use of large-language models for text generation and text classification tasks.

Selected outlets

My research has been published in the American Journal of Political Science, British Journal of Political Science, Political Communication, Legislative Studies Quarterly, and Electoral Politics.

Award

“Be Careful What You Wish For: The Impacts of President Trump’s Midterm Endorsements”, with Andrew Ballard and Hans Hassell, won the 2023 Jewell–Loewenberg Prize for the best article in American Politics from the Legislative Politics section of the American Political Science Association.
