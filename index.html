Hello

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Sociology at the University of Oxford, where I work on the DemDiologue project. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Amsterdam on the Horizon-funded TWON (Twin of Online Networks) project.

My research focuses on political communication, political institutions, political behavior, and electoral politics. Substantively, I study communication strategies used by political elites and how voters understand and process political communication. Methodologically, I build large and novel datasets and use machine learning, natural language processing, and large language models for quantitative text analysis.

I received my PhD in Political Science from American University in 2022. My dissertation, Persuaded by the Polls: The Effects of Election Polling on Individual Electoral Behavior, examined how election polling affects individual electoral behavior.

My work has appeared in journals including the American Journal of Political Science, British Journal of Political Science, Political Communication, American Journal of Political Science, Legislative Studies Quarterly, and Electoral Studies, as well as a forthcoming Cambridge University Press book chapter on artificial intelligence, politics, and political science.

Current interests: political communication, electoral politics, elite rhetoric, online political behavior, text analysis, machine learning, and the use of large language models in social science research.
