Hello

I am post-doctoral researcher at the University of Oxford working on the DemDiologue project. I was formerly a post-doctoral researcher at the University of Amsterdam working on the Horizon-funded project TWON (Twin of Online Networks). I received my PhD in Political Science from American University in 2022.

My research focuses on political communication, political institutions, political behavior, and electoral politics. Substantively, I study communication strategies used by political elites and how voters understand and process political communication. Methodologically, I build large and novel datasets and use machine learning, natural language processing, and large language models for quantitative text analysis.

My research has been published in outlets such as the American Journal of Political Science, the British Journal of Political Science, Political Communication, and Legislative Studies Quarterly, and has been featured in popular media outlets such as The Washington Post, Fivethirtyeight, and NBC News. My article "Be Careful What You Wish For: The Impacts of President Trump’s Midterm Endorsements" with Andrew Ballard and Hans Hassell won the 2023 Jewell-Loewenberg Prize for the best article in american Politics from the Legislative Politics section of the American Political science Association.
